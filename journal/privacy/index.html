Privacy Policy

Markets of Tomorrow · Florida of Tomorrow LLC · Last updated July 17, 2026

Markets of Tomorrow ("we," "us") operates oftmw.com and its related outlets and services. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create a free or paid member account, we collect your name and email address to operate your membership.
  • Newsletter subscriptions. If you subscribe, we store your email address to deliver the newsletter. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Usage analytics. We collect standard, aggregated analytics about how our pages are used (page views, referral sources, approximate region) to improve the site.
  • Inquiries. If you contact us or submit a partnership form, we keep the details you provide so we can respond.
  • Business accounting data. Internally, we connect our own accounting systems (such as QuickBooks) to reconcile our own invoices and payments. This concerns our business records only and involves no reader or member data.

How we use information

  • To operate memberships, newsletters, and site features you request.
  • To understand readership and improve our coverage.
  • To respond to inquiries and manage advertising and partnership relationships.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties except the service providers that run our infrastructure (hosting, email delivery, payments, analytics), and only as needed to operate the service.

Payments

Paid memberships are processed by our payment providers (such as Stripe). We never see or store your full card details.

Data retention & your choices

We keep account and subscription data for as long as your account or subscription is active. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, and you can request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored with reputable cloud providers. Access to member data is limited to the small team that operates Markets of Tomorrow.
