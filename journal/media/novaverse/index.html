How it works

A snippet, a field, and it is live.

Comet is already built for this. It streams in the browser, needs no download, and embeds with a code snippet. On our side a twin is one field on the project record, and every surface reads it.

1

You send the embed

A Comet URL or snippet per project, plus a poster frame for the tile.

2

We attach it

Stored on the project record and picked up by map, project page, Atlas and Onyx.

3

It goes live

Within a build cycle. Credit line renders automatically on every surface.

4

Both sides see the numbers

Impressions, opens and clicks per surface, reported monthly to you and your client.
