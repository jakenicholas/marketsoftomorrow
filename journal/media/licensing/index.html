How Licensing Is Scoped

Priced by permission,
not by audience

A license is not metered by how many people see the work. You're charged for the scope of permission — the categories of use you're entitled to. Scope is defined by three levers.

Lever 01
Media
Where the work may appear — organic social, owned channels, paid media, print, out-of-home, third-party OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia), and brand / portfolio reuse.
Lever 02
Term
How long the work may be used. The free Social license runs ongoing; a purchased photo is yours to use in perpetuity — no term, no renewal.
Lever 03
Exclusivity
Whether the Studio may license comparable frames of the same property to other parties. Per-property exclusivity is available on request.

Two simple lanes: share freely on social, or license a photo once and it works everywhere your marketing lives.
