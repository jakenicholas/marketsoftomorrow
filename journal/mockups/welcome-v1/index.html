What's being proposed. Every lightbox in the funnel (email capture, account nudge, Go Pro) is replaced with full-screen, app-grade moments. The gate replaces the slide-up tile at the same trigger points; after account creation, the member number celebration and the Pro pitch run as screens 2 and 3 of one continuous flow instead of separate popups on later visits.

A · Cinematic: full-bleed rotating project imagery, wordmark top-left, live network stats on the right. Feels like the cover of the product. Strongest brand moment; the form floats over the image so it needs the dark gradient to stay legible.

B · Split panel: imagery left, calm form pane right with perks list and a step indicator ("Step 1 of 2" sets the expectation there's a Pro pitch coming, which raises conversion honesty). Most conventional, easiest to trust with a password field. On phones the panel goes full-bleed.

C · Guided steps: app-onboarding language with progress dots, one idea per screen, centered. The most mobile-native of the three; the dots make the Pro screen feel like part of setup rather than an ad.

Shared decisions to settle: (1) hard gate vs the "Not now" escape hatch top-right; keeping an escape means it's a strong nudge rather than a wall. (2) The celebration screen turns the member number into the product's hero moment; it also makes the Pro pitch feel earned rather than interruptive. (3) Pricing shows the new $32/$300 with annual preselected. (4) All screens are the same component with different content, so shipping this is one overlay, not four.
