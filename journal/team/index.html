Jake Nicholas is the founder and CEO of Markets of Tomorrow, a global journal and intelligence platform tracking the world’s most significant new developments in hospitality, residential, golf, and dining. He built the company around a simple thesis: the most valuable real estate stories are the ones told before the ribbon is cut.

Based in West Palm Beach, Florida, Nicholas started in his home market and grew Markets of Tomorrow outward into a global operation. It pairs a real-time journal with a database of 1,000+ daily tracked projects worldwide, an interactive Map and data Atlas, and TMW Intelligence, its proprietary AI research layer built for predictive forecasting, natural-language search, and comparable-project analysis.

Today the platform amasses over 10 million views monthly. Nicholas leads product, partnerships, and global coverage across luxury hospitality and real estate.

About Markets of Tomorrow

Tracking what the world builds next.

Markets of Tomorrow is a real-time journal and data platform tracking the world’s most significant new developments in hospitality, residential, golf, and dining.

A global journal backed by a real-time database of 1,000+ daily tracked projects worldwide, powered by an interactive Map, data Atlas, and our proprietary AI research layer, TMW Intelligence, built for predictive forecasting, natural-language search, and comparable-project analysis.

We amass over 10 million views monthly, with focus markets in Florida, New York, Tennessee, the Caribbean, and the Rockies, plus Hotels of Tomorrow, our global travel arm.
