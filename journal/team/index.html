Jake Nicholas

Jake Nicholas is the founder and CEO of Markets of Tomorrow, a luxury real estate development intelligence platform tracking what’s being built next across hospitality, residential, golf, and dining. Based in West Palm Beach, Florida, he built the company around a simple thesis: the most valuable real estate stories are the ones told before the ribbon is cut.

Under Nicholas, Markets of Tomorrow has grown into two connected products. The editorial arm covers marquee developments across South Florida, West Palm Beach and Miami above all, alongside notable projects worldwide. The Map of Tomorrow, the company’s interactive development-tracking tool, pairs that coverage with TMW Intelligence, a predictive engine that forecasts delivery timelines from comparable projects.

Nicholas leads editorial, product, and the company’s paid-media work with luxury hospitality and real estate clients.

About Markets of Tomorrow

Tracking what the world builds next.

Markets of Tomorrow is a real-time journal and data platform tracking the world’s most significant new developments in hospitality, residential, golf, and dining.

A global journal backed by a real-time database of 1,000+ daily tracked projects worldwide, powered by an interactive Map, data Atlas, and our proprietary AI research layer, TMW Intelligence, built for predictive forecasting, natural-language search, and comparable-project analysis.

We reach 8M+ people a month, with focus markets in Florida, New York, Tennessee, the Caribbean, and the Rockies, plus Hotels of Tomorrow, our global travel arm.
