About the project

The Standard Residences, Brickell is a 46-story luxury condominium tower at 99 SW 7th Street on the Miami River, in the heart of Brickell, Miami's financial district. Developed by Newgard Group and Two Roads Development as one of the twin towers of One Brickell Riverfront alongside LOFTY Brickell, it is designed by Arquitectonica with interiors by GOODRICH and holds 422 fully furnished studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom residences. The building extends The Standard hotel brand's design-led, social approach to condo living: amenities include a rooftop pool deck, The Standard Club Lounge and Cafe, coworking spaces, a tenth-floor spa with hammam, saunas and cold plunge, a bowling alley, screening room, pet spa and signature dining venues, with riverfront and skyline views throughout.
