Eudēmonia returns to West Palm Beach this November, bigger than ever

Eudēmonia, the health, well-being, and longevity summit from the team behind Wanderlust, returns to West Palm Beach November 5–8, 2026, with 120+ experts, 200+ talks, and 300 treatments.

By Jake Nicholas July 6, 2026

Eudēmonia is returning to West Palm Beach this fall, and it is bigger than ever. Running November 5–8, 2026 at the Palm Beach County Convention Center and venues across downtown, the health, well-being, and longevity summit, built by the team behind Wanderlust, has become one of the most talked-about wellness events in the country. This year's theme says it plainly: wellness is a team sport.

The 2026 edition brings together more than 120 experts across 200-plus talks, 160-plus brands, 15 venues, and 300 treatments, all over three days. Think of it as less a conference than a retreat crossed with a festival, a place where you might move from a talk on longevity to a breathwork session to a cold plunge to a live DJ set, then close the day in conversation with the person next to you. It is prevention-focused and science-based by design, with a stated mission to cut through wellness fads and misinformation and keep every session grounded in real research.

The programming spans the full frontier of modern health: longevity and aging, hormonal health, metabolic and gut health, brain and neurological health, biohacking, peptides, psychedelic-assisted therapy, sleep, stress recovery, nutrition, and mindset optimization, among dozens of other tracks. Past editions have drawn headliners like neuroscientist Andrew Huberman, functional-medicine pioneer Mark Hyman, muscle-health expert Dr. Gabrielle Lyon, and endurance athlete and podcaster Rich Roll, and the 2026 lineup, unveiling in waves, is on track to be the deepest yet, with more than 150 experts confirmed and over 100 still to be announced.

Beyond the main stages, the summit's signature experiences return. The EXPOSŌME, its curated expo floor, gathers the most interesting brands in biometric testing, wearables, personalized medicine, and wellness technology under one roof, routinely cited by attendees as one of the best expos in the space.

The Health Innovation Lab spotlights what is coming next, and a full slate of movement classes, restorative treatments, and spa experiences runs throughout, so the days are as hands-on as they are informative. With more than 1,200 five-star reviews behind it, Eudēmonia has built the rare event that people describe less like a work trip and more like coming home.

For anyone in South Florida who takes their health seriously, or wants to, this is the one to circle on the calendar. Eudēmonia 2026 runs November 5–8 in West Palm Beach, and badges are on sale now.
